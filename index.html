Optional email connections

Bill updates,
with your permission.

Google or Microsoft sign-in identifies your BearDen profile. Reading email requires a separate permission that you choose to grant.

With Gmail access, BearDen searches for bill and payment notices, reads matching messages, and extracts details such as amounts, due dates and payment status. You can also select a message to link it to a bill or debt.

Gmail access is read-only. BearDen does not send, modify or delete your messages. Disconnect a mailbox in Settings or revoke its access through your Google Account.
