1. About this policy

BearDen is a personal household-finance application maintained by Kurtis Harrison. This policy covers the BearDen application at bearden.kurtismharrison.workers.dev and its public information pages, hosted on Cloudflare and GitHub Pages.

BearDen organizes bills, debt, income, savings and payment information. It does not initiate bank transfers or pay bills. Connections to email, banks and supported rent portals are optional.

2. Information BearDen processes

  • Sign-in information: your Google or Microsoft account identifier and email address, used to identify your BearDen profile and restore its saved information.
  • Information you enter: budgets, bills, debts, income settings, savings goals, notes, preferences, household contributions and payment-matching rules.
  • Connected financial information: account names and identifiers, balances, transactions, and available loan or credit-card details received from the providers you connect.
  • Email information: connected mailbox addresses, matching message content processed to identify bills and payments, and saved details such as sender, subject, message identifiers, dates, amounts, payment references and partial account numbers. Google-specific use is described below.
  • Rent-portal information: portal address, balance readings and session information. If you enable automatic portal sign-in, BearDen also stores the portal credentials you provide.
  • Statement insights: supported statement files are parsed in your browser. Reviewed figures and report settings are saved to your BearDen profile; the original uploaded statement file is not sent to BearDen’s server by this feature.
  • Support requests: the report text you submit, your profile identifier, app version, browser information and screen size, used to investigate bugs and feature requests.

BearDen uses this information to provide the features you choose, save and restore your plan, match bills and payments, refresh connections, calculate estimates and respond to support requests.

3. Google and Gmail data

Basic Google sign-in requests identity information. Gmail reading is a separate, optional authorization using the gmail.readonly permission. This permission technically allows reading your mailbox; BearDen uses it to search for and read bill, statement and payment notices, including messages you select or senders you link to a bill or debt.

Matching message content is processed on BearDen’s server to extract relevant financial details. BearDen stores those extracted details and message metadata so it can show email updates and match notices to your bills, debts and bank activity. It does not persist full message bodies in its parsed-message cache. Saved authorization tokens allow checks to continue while the app is closed.

BearDen does not send, modify or delete Gmail messages. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Google user data is used and transferred only as necessary to provide or improve the user-facing features you authorize, for security, to comply with applicable law, or otherwise as allowed by Google’s Limited Use requirements. Human access to Google user data is limited to cases allowed by those requirements, such as your affirmative agreement for specific support or a necessary security or legal purpose.

BearDen’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and protection

Signed-in profile records and connection credentials are stored on BearDen’s Cloudflare backend. Sensitive stored application records, including provider tokens and optional rent credentials, are encrypted before database storage. Browser connections use HTTPS and sign-in sessions use protected cookies.

Profiles are separated by sign-in identity. Household planning settings do not automatically merge two people’s private profiles; an explicit sharing or importing action can copy information you choose to share. Device-only use and some preferences can be stored in your browser. Use a device you trust and clear its website data when appropriate.

Technical protections reduce risk but cannot guarantee that any online service is completely secure. BearDen is a personal project, and saved current state is not a versioned backup service.

5. Providers and limited sharing

Cloudflare hosts the application and its stored data. Google and Microsoft provide sign-in and optional email access. Plaid, Lunch Flow and a supported rent portal are involved only when you use those connections. They process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The application may load fonts from Google Fonts.

BearDen’s public information pages are hosted on Cloudflare and GitHub Pages. GitHub and other hosting providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses, browser information and access times to deliver and protect their services. This information website uses no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by BearDen. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Support reports are available to the app maintainer and, when enabled by the maintainer, an authorized support assistant used to investigate the reported issue. Only include information needed for your request; do not include passwords, access tokens or full account numbers.

Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising. Information may be disclosed when necessary to respond to your request, operate the features described here, address abuse or security problems, or comply with a legal obligation. Google user data remains subject to the additional restrictions above.

6. Retention, controls and deletion

Your saved plan remains in your profile until you remove it or request deletion. Connection data is retained while needed for the features you use. Cached email results are bounded, while extracted information saved into your bills or debts can remain part of your plan.

  • Disconnect a mailbox: use Settings → Email accounts → Disconnect. This removes BearDen’s saved mailbox connection and its cached parsed messages. Gmail disconnection also requests revocation of its Google authorization.
  • Revoke Google access: visit your Google Account connections and remove BearDen’s access. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase information previously saved in your BearDen plan.
  • Disconnect another provider: use the corresponding connection controls in Settings. Manually entered bills, debts and other plan records may remain after disconnection.
  • Remove device data: clear BearDen’s website storage in your browser or device settings. This does not delete the signed-in server profile.
  • Request account or data deletion: email kurtismharrison@gmail.com from the address associated with your profile. Specify whether you want your whole BearDen profile or particular stored information removed. The maintainer may verify your identity before handling the request. There is currently no one-click account-deletion control.

Deletion from BearDen does not delete records held independently by your bank, email provider, or other connected service. Data required by applicable law or needed to resolve an active security issue may be retained for that purpose.

7. Questions and updates

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact Kurtis Harrison at kurtismharrison@gmail.com. Do not email account passwords or authentication codes.

This policy may be updated as BearDen changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes to Google data use will be disclosed and additional consent requested when required.
