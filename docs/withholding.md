# Paycheck withholding estimates

The planner uses a 26-paycheck year (every two weeks). Its 2026 federal calculation follows Worksheet 1A and the annual percentage tables in [IRS Publication 15-T](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t). It supports the 2020-or-later W-4 filing statuses, Step 2(c), Step 3 dollar credits, Steps 4(a–c), and an explicit federal exemption. Selecting two jobs uses the IRS checkbox tables; it does not guess a flat extra percentage or change Michigan’s state rate. Users should match their actual W-4; the checkbox method is intended for two jobs and works best for similar pay. More complex situations can use the [IRS estimator](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator).

Michigan is the currently implemented automatic state calculation: 4.25%, less $5,900 annually per MI-W4 exemption, from [Michigan Treasury’s 2026 tables](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/business-taxes/withholding/calendar-year-tax-information) and the [NFC Michigan formula](https://help.nfc.usda.gov/bulletins/2026/1781704387.htm). Other states do not receive a guessed tax estimate; use actual take-home pay. Exemptions default to zero until the user enters their own MI-W4 election. Federal and state exemptions are independent.

[IRS Publication 15](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15) supplies Social Security (6.2% up to $184,500) and Medicare (1.45%, plus 0.9% on this employer’s wages above $200,000). Optional year-to-date taxable wages are before the check being estimated; zero is a starting assumption, not a known wage history. Pre-tax health deductions reduce income and FICA wages; traditional retirement deductions reduce income-tax wages only. Optional local tax and other deductions are dollar amounts per paycheck. No local rate, reciprocity, special payroll exemption, tax refund, or final tax liability is inferred.

New bonuses use the separately identified supplemental-pay estimate: 22% federal plus Michigan and FICA, subject to the saved exemption and wage settings. Employers can use a different permitted method. The estimator does not support supplemental wages over $1 million in a year; confirm the payroll amount for those cases. Already saved bonuses remain net amounts and are not taxed again. Existing saved take-home pay remains authoritative until the user explicitly chooses Use This Estimate. A tax-year label remains visible; the app does not silently relabel 2026 tables as later-year tables.

These settings estimate cash flow only and do not submit or change an employer W-4 or MI-W4.
