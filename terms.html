You retain your rights to information you provide. You authorize BearDen to process it as described in the Privacy policy to deliver the features you choose. Do not use the app to access another person’s data without permission, interfere with the service, or engage in unlawful activity.

3. Estimates and payment responsibility

Forecasts, payoff dates, interest calculations, income estimates and payment matches can be incomplete, delayed or incorrect. Connected providers may not supply every account, transaction or balance update. Review important information with your bank, creditor, employer or biller before acting.

BearDen does not pay bills or move money. Recording a payment, marking a bill paid or planning an allocation in BearDen does not submit a payment. You remain responsible for making payments and meeting your obligations.
